Joining Softnet Technology Academy.

We keep the process simple and personal — a conversation and a campus visit, not just a form.

The process

Four steps from enquiry to enrolment.

1

Enquiry

Send the form below or call the school office with your child's age and the term you're hoping to join.

2

Campus visit

Tour the classrooms and labs, meet a member of staff, and see a typical school day in session.

3

Assessment

A short, age-appropriate placement assessment in literacy and numeracy, used to place your child correctly — not to gatekeep.

4

Enrolment

Complete registration, receive your welcome pack and uniform list, and confirm your child's start date.

Good to know

Entry requirements & documents.

For Early Years and Primary: a completed application form, the child's birth certificate, immunisation record, and two passport photographs.

For Secondary entry (JSS1 and above): the above documents plus the most recent school report and, where applicable, a transfer letter from the previous school.

Mid-term transfers are considered on a case-by-case basis, subject to space in the relevant class and a placement assessment.

Fees are payable per term and cover tuition, learning materials for the technology strand, and continuous assessment. A detailed fee schedule is shared directly with families at enquiry stage — please contact the school office for current figures.
