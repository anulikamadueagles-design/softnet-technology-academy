Home / About

A school started with one question: what does a Nigerian child actually need to thrive next?

Softnet Technology Academy exists to answer that question every single day — in Asokoro Extension, Abuja.

Our mission

Raise learners who read well, reason well, and build with technology.

Nigeria doesn't lack talented children. It lacks enough schools willing to combine a serious academic foundation with real, hands-on technology skills, taught from an early age rather than bolted on as an afterthought.

Softnet Technology Academy was founded to close that gap — to give children in Abuja a school where strong English and Mathematics results sit naturally alongside coding, robotics and design, because that is the world they are actually growing up into.

Our vision

Every graduate leaves ready — for further study, for work, for whatever comes next.

We measure ourselves by what our learners can do, not only by what they can recite. A Softnet graduate should be able to write a clear paragraph, solve a real problem with mathematics, and build something functional with technology — a website, a simple program, a working prototype.

That combination — literacy, numeracy and technical fluency — is what we believe every Nigerian child deserves the chance to build.

How we work

Four commitments that shape every school day.

  • Small classes, close attentionTeachers who know each learner's name, strengths and struggles — and adjust for them, not just the syllabus.
  • Technology as a language, not a subjectCoding and digital skills run through the timetable from Primary onward, alongside — never instead of — core academics.
  • Character built through routineConsistent expectations around punctuality, respect and effort, reinforced daily rather than only spoken about.
  • Parents kept genuinely informedRegular updates and an open door, so no family is surprised at the end of term.
Our people

Teachers who chose to teach this way on purpose.

Every member of staff at Softnet is hired for two things: mastery of their subject, and a genuine ability to connect with children.

Recruitment

Subject-qualified, carefully selected

Our teachers hold recognised qualifications in the subjects they teach, and are selected as much for patience and clarity as for credentials.

Development

Trained in modern teaching methods

Ongoing training keeps staff current on both pedagogy and the technology skills our curriculum demands.

Care

Pastoral support built in

Form teachers track each learner's wellbeing and progress closely, and flag concerns to parents early rather than late.
