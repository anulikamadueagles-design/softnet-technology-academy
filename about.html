What we do

Softnet Technology Academy is a tech training academy based in Abuja, Nigeria. We teach web development, AI and prompt engineering, ethical hacking, graphics design and ways to make money with tech.

Our approach is simple: learn it, build it, use it. Every course is built around real projects, so students leave with work they can show to clients and employers, not only theory.

How we teach

Practical first

We explain only what you need, then you try it straight away.

Real projects

You build websites, designs and security checks that look like real work.

Ethics matter

We teach security skills to protect people and systems, always with permission and within the law.
