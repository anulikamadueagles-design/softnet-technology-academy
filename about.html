About Softnet Technology Academy

Softnet Technology Academy is a tech training academy based in Abuja, Nigeria. Our goal is simple: to teach practical tech skills that people can use immediately to build websites, protect systems, create designs and earn income.

We focus on hands-on learning. Every course is built around real projects, so students leave with skills they can show to clients and employers, not just theory.
