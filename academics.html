Home / Academics

A curriculum that takes both books and computers seriously.

Every stage at Softnet Technology Academy combines national curriculum academics with a genuine technology strand — not a once-a-week novelty, but a running thread through the whole school day.

Early Years · Ages 3–5

Where curiosity gets its first structure.

Our Early Years classrooms build the basics that every later subject depends on: spoken language, early literacy and number sense, delivered through play, story and guided activity rather than worksheets alone.

Simple pattern, sorting and sequencing games — the earliest form of computational thinking — are woven naturally into daily play, so logical thinking starts long before a child sees a keyboard.

Small group sizes mean every child is seen and heard. Our Early Years teachers are trained specifically in early-childhood development, and the day is structured around the attention span of a three-, four- or five-year-old — not an adult timetable.

Primary · Ages 6–11

A strong core, plus a weekly computing lesson that actually builds things.

English Studies, Mathematics, Basic Science and Social Studies form the academic core, taught to a standard that prepares learners confidently for the transition into Secondary school.

Alongside that core, every Primary class has dedicated computing time in our lab — typing and digital literacy in the earliest years, moving into block-based programming, then simple text-based coding and beginner robotics as learners progress through Primary 4 to 6.

By the end of Primary school, a Softnet learner can write a well-structured essay, solve multi-step maths problems confidently, and has already built a working program or robot of their own — a head start most children don't get until much later, if at all.

Secondary · JSS1 – SSS3

Full WAEC/NECO-aligned academics, deepened by a live technology strand.

Junior Secondary learners cover the full national curriculum across sciences, humanities and business studies, building toward the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).

In Senior Secondary, learners choose a stream — Sciences, Arts, or Commercial — and work toward WAEC and NECO examinations with focused revision support, past-question practice and regular internal assessment to track progress.

Running alongside every stream is our technology strand: structured programming, data handling, and digital design lessons that continue right through to SSS3, so technical fluency grows in step with academic depth rather than stopping after Primary school.

Tech Track · Elective, from JSS1

For learners who want to go further.

An advanced, optional pathway for learners with a strong pull toward building things.

  1. I

    Software development

    Web & app basics

    Learners move from block-based logic to real programming languages, building websites, small applications and games they can demonstrate and explain.

  2. II

    Robotics & electronics

    Hands-on hardware

    Circuit basics, sensors and simple robotics kits, used to design and build working models that solve a defined problem.

  3. III

    Design & UI/UX

    Digital design

    The basics of good digital design — layout, colour, usability — applied to real projects rather than taught only in theory.

  4. IV

    Entrepreneurship

    From idea to pitch

    Learners take a project from concept to a short pitch, practising the communication and planning skills that turn a good idea into something real.
