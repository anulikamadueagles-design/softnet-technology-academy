The prospectus

One school, one pathway, four stages.

Every stage builds fluency in literacy, numeracy and technology together — not as separate subjects, but as one way of thinking.

  1. I

    Early Years

    Ages 3–5

    Play-based learning that builds language, number sense and curiosity, with early exposure to simple logic and pattern games that lay the groundwork for computational thinking.

  2. II

    Primary

    Ages 6–11

    A strong grounding in the national curriculum — English, Mathematics, Basic Science — alongside weekly computing lessons where pupils build their first programs, websites and simple robots.

  3. III

    Secondary

    Ages 12–16 · JSS & SSS

    Full WAEC/NECO-aligned academics in the sciences, arts and commercial subjects, deepened by a technology strand covering programming, data and digital design that runs through every term.

  4. IV

    Tech Track

    Elective, from JSS1

    An advanced elective for learners who want to go further — software development, robotics, UI/UX design and entrepreneurship — building a portfolio they can show before they graduate.

What we hold ourselves to.

Our promise

Academic excellence, without the narrowness

We take the core curriculum seriously — strong results in English, Mathematics and the sciences are non-negotiable. Technology is added to that foundation, never a substitute for it.

Class size

Small enough to notice

Small class sizes mean teachers catch a struggling learner early, and stretch a fast one further, term after term.

Character

Discipline with warmth

Clear expectations, consistent routines, and a staff who know every learner's name and how they're really doing.

Safety

A secure, well-run campus

Controlled access, trained staff, and a calm daily routine that parents can rely on without a second thought.

Community

Parents as partners

Regular reporting, open office hours and a staff who welcome a phone call — not just a termly report card.

Readiness

Built for what's next

On campus

A campus made for hands-on learning.

Computer & robotics lab

A dedicated lab with individual workstations, robotics kits and project space, where classes build real software and hardware projects rather than only reading about them.

Library & reading room

A quiet, well-stocked room that pairs printed books with a digital reading programme, building the reading habit every other subject depends on.

Science & art studios

Purpose-built spaces for practical science and creative work, so learners test ideas with their hands, not only in exercise books.

Sport & play

Supervised outdoor play and organised sport keep the day balanced — because well-rounded learners perform better in the classroom too.
