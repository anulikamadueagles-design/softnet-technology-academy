Courses that teach by building

Five practical courses, from 2 to 8 weeks. Choose one to start, or combine them as you grow.

Web Development

Learn to design and build responsive websites from scratch with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. You work on real pages from the first week and finish with a site you can show to clients.

  • Page structure and clean HTML
  • Styling and mobile-first layouts with CSS
  • JavaScript basics, menus and forms
  • Publishing your site online

You finish with: a live website you built yourself.

AI & Prompt Engineering

Understand how AI tools work and how to get reliable results from them. You learn to write clear prompts for research, writing, design and business tasks.

  • Writing clear, specific prompts
  • Using AI tools for research and content
  • Checking AI answers for mistakes
  • Turning AI skills into useful services

Ethical Hacking

Learn how attackers think and how websites and devices are protected. You practise finding weaknesses legally, with permission, and fixing them.

  • How networks and websites get attacked
  • Finding weaknesses in a safe, legal way
  • Securing accounts, devices and sites
  • Ethics, permission and the law

You finish with: the skills to test and secure systems responsibly.
